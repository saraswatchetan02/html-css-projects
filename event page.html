GLA Tech Fest

Introduction

Techनव्या (Technavya) 2025 was the premier annual technical festival of GLA University, held at the Mathura campus from February 7–8, 2025. Organized by the Office of Students' Welfare, the festival was themed around "Space and Technology" under the tagline "Pioneering the Tech Cosmos".
